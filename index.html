About Me

I am a 2nd-year M.Phil. student in the Red Bird Program (Artificial Intelligence) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (Guangzhou), under the supervision of Prof. Xuming HU and Prof. Yangqiu SONG. I will become a visiting student in King Abdullah University of Science and Technology under the supervision of Prof. Mohamed Elhoseiny from Sep. 2026. My current research interest focuses on the panoramic world, mainly exploring the potential of panoramas to be a new bridge towards real embodied intelligence and world model. I work closely with Prof. Xu ZHENG @ GBU, Prof. Linfeng ZHANG @ SJTU, and Prof. Nicu Sebe @ UniTrento.
